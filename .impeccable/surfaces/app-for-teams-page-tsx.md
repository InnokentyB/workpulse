# For Teams commercial presentation

- Scope: `/for-teams`, a persuasive commercial one-pager inside the established WorkPulse visual system.
- Audience: HR and People Operations leaders evaluating a low-friction workplace wellbeing pilot.
- Job: understand the cost of badly timed reminders, see why WorkPulse is different, and decide whether a limited team pilot is worth discussing.
- Primary action: open the working decision demo. Secondary action: inspect the pilot and pricing hypothesis.
- Proof: the live `MOVE NOW` / `NOT NOW` demo, deterministic explainable logic, and optional on-device verification. No customer, clinical, ROI, or validated pricing claims.
- Story: interruption cost → contextual decision mechanism → value for employees and People Ops → pilot → monetization hypothesis → validation metrics.
- Chosen structure: an “interruption ledger” that treats attention as the scarce resource and shows WorkPulse balancing movement need against interruption cost.
- Memorable moment: the opening comparison where a generic timer interrupts two minutes before a meeting while WorkPulse explicitly holds.
- Constraints: preserve the incumbent paper/ink/signal visual system, equal authority for MOVE and HOLD, medical-claim restraint, 360px support, keyboard accessibility, and reduced motion.
- Open decisions: willingness to pay, package boundaries, buyer procurement path, pilot conversion, integrations, and reporting permissions.
